## Platform

web

## Users

- Students looking for a strong peer community and ways to connect through technology-centered events and collaboration.
- Companies and industry teams interested in exploring collaboration with the student community.

## Product Purpose

DevCatalyst is a student community focused on building strong relationships among students through events, shared learning, and collaboration. Success means students feel connected to one another and have opportunities to engage with outside collaborators.

## Positioning

DevCatalyst is a community and convening point for students and collaborators, not primarily a software product or a membership funnel.

## Operating Context

The website should help students understand the community and its events, while giving companies and industry teams a clear way to explore collaboration.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Event and workshop collaboration is a confirmed area of interest.
- Potential partner conversations may also cover speakers, mentors, and industry challenges. These are invitations to explore, not claims that an established program exists.
- Joining the community is one path for students, but should remain secondary to the broader community story.

## Product Principles

- Lead with community and relationships.
- Show events and collaboration as ways people connect.
- Make student, event, and partner paths clear without treating membership as the whole purpose.
- Use real, verifiable examples as proof; label possibilities as possibilities.
